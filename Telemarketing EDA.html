Data-Driven Telemarketing Strategy for Portuguese Bank Term Deposits: An Exploratory Analysis of the UCI Bank Marketing Dataset¶

1. Background of the company.¶

PortuBank is a Portuguese retail bank that has €2.8 billion in assets under management (Banco de Portugal 2025). It has a focus on term deposit yet the growth has been deteriorating by -8% YoY. With the increase of Euribor benchmark rates, customers are switching to higher yield products (European Money Markets Institute 2024).

1.2 Organizational Challenges of Businesses:¶

2025 Crisis: A telemarketing outlay of €1.2 million converted just 11.7%, or in other words, 4,800 retail clients from 41,000 calls. Each deposit that leads to a success is about €8,500. However, the campaign costs €250/client. This means that the ROI is only just 1.8x.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS:¶

  1. Which job segments are most likely to convert to deposits? (Target high-return jobs)
  2. Does campaign timing affect the success rate? (Plan scheduling correctly)
  3. What impact does the economic confidence index have on conversions? (macro timing) (European Commission DG ECFIN 2026).
  4. Which customer profiles have been successful in previous campaigns? (VIP cultivation)
  5. Do longer calls indicate increased likelihood of subscription? (Achievement threshold)
  6. Which contact types perform better: cellular or telephone? (Distribution of the channels)
  7. What is the ideal number of contacts for campaign fatigue? (Cost-Effectiveness)
  8. Which age group responds most effectively to the term deposit offer? (Public Health)
  9. Does one's relationship status impact willingness to subscribe? (Targeting Families)
  10. What time thresholds suggest high-probability leads? (Scoring in real-time)

1.3. Data set:¶

UCI Bank Marketing – 41,188 campaigns done by Portuguese banks (20 different features) 11.7% term deposit conversion (Moro, Rita and Cortez 2014; sahistapatel96 2021). Dataset Link : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sahistapatel96/bankadditionalfullcsv
